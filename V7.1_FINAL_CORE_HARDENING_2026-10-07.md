- Existing Capability Registry and Model Router remain in place; no provider feature creep is introduced.

## Architectural rule

Gemini may generate or explain, but it is not the authority for MAIA local state. A local state-changing operation must be classified, safety-checked, executed by a MAIA capability, verified against storage, and only then reported as successful.

## Scope discipline

No calendar, contacts, WhatsApp, calls, camera, screen sharing, monetization, or unrelated UI feature is introduced in this hardening cycle. Those remain roadmap items for later phases.
